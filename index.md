## BA Philosophy

I see the BA role as translation: taking complex, nuanced requirements from subject-matter experts and turning them into clear, actionable input for technical delivery teams. That translation is a craft as much as a process — the differentiator is simplifying without losing context. A strong BA absorbs and resolves ambiguity rather than passing it downstream; if the delivery team inherits my open questions, I haven't done the job. All of it rests on rapport across every stakeholder level, which is why I position myself deliberately at the bridge between senior leadership, technical delivery, and day-to-day operations.

## Working AI-augmented

An AI-augmented BA is judged on the same output as any other BA — the difference is cycle time. I use AI to accelerate documentation and analysis drafting, then verify against source data before anything reaches a stakeholder. Verification is the non-negotiable: an unchecked AI output is an open question passed downstream, which is exactly what a BA shouldn't do.
