# About / Experience

**Four years** across regulated care, financial services, and commercial
operations — requirements elicitation, process mapping, KPI and metric
definition, and reconciliation design, delivered through Power BI, advanced
Excel, and Power Query. My work sits where operational process and data meet:
reconciling what a process is supposed to do against what the data says it
actually did, then building the reporting that keeps the gap visible.

Currently a Business Analyst at a CQC-regulated UK domiciliary care provider,
bridging leadership, operations, and system vendors. That client's identity and
its commissioning authority are withheld across this site by design — sector,
scale, and scope are given in full, and they are what the work is evidenced by.
